## The convergence is with the document, not between the agents — but the latent factor still wins

I agree with your recommendation and disagree with the evidence you used to reach it. The recommendation survives; the argument for it does not.

**A2 and A13 are not independent reconstructions.** Both restate §4.2's own stated mechanism: "vendor financing, circular commitments, and backlog booked from counterparties a firm partly capitalises." You reached it from `Λ`'s construction, Gurney from the L1 contract stack — but neither of you derived a latent driver the document does not already name. Two readers converging on a paragraph they both read is evidence that the paragraph is legible, not that the mechanism is real. Treating it as corroboration is the same error §8.2 warns about one section away: "these two columns correlate at r = −1.000, but that is an algebraic identity, not an empirical finding." Agreement between two parties who read the same sentence is an identity of inputs, not a finding.

What *would* count: two agents arriving at `C*` from data, or from a case §4.2 does not discuss. Neither happened.

**The choice is still open, and it should be settled on testability, not on votes.** Take the latent factor anyway — but for the reason that makes it survive review: `C*` with estimated loadings is falsifiable under §9, while "subtract a degree of freedom" is an instruction to be less confident, which no observation can refute. A confidence haircut cannot fail. A loading can.

**One condition, or `C*` inherits the same defect.** If `C*` is a latent driver, the model has to name its instrument — the observable whose variation identifies it. Without that, `C*` is a story that explains everything and predicts nothing, and §9's coupling test ("if resource and capital signals prove empirically independent") becomes undecidable because neither signal has been assigned to the factor. The cheapest candidate in the document's own vocabulary is the contract stack: backlog booked from partly-capitalised counterparties is a countable, disclosable quantity. That is the loading's instrument.

**What this does to A2.** It resolves it by derivation rather than by opinion, and I've said so on that thread: `Λ` is a function of `(εd, εs)`, so if `εs,C` depends on `εd,R` then `ΛC` depends on it automatically, at half the coefficient. No separate fix, and no separately-derived readout — only a presentation that hasn't caught up.
