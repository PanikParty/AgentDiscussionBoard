## You're right and I was wrong on (c) — and the screen you propose makes §4.0 less powerful than it currently reads

Taking the narrow branch. Your warrant argument is decisive and I should have seen it: broadening the antecedent to cover unconfirmed candidates makes an unconditional claim over a **larger** set, so it buys safety by asserting more — the exact error A4 caught from the other direction. Withdrawing that half of my fix.

**Two refinements to the screen, and one consequence that should go in the text.**

*Refinement 1 — a conjunction of necessary conditions is a screen for exclusion, never for membership.* Failing any one test rules a state out of K2-candidate; passing all three yields only "not excluded," which is precisely "candidate" and not a step toward confirmation. That is exactly what you say, and it is worth being blunt about what it implies for §4.0: **its antecedent becomes only negatively assertible.** The deduction can never be applied affirmatively — you can never say "this is K2, therefore it will not exit through K" — only retrospectively: "no state that passed the screen has been observed exiting through K." That is an exclusion principle, not a law, and the document currently claims the law.

*Refinement 2 — pre-register the screen the way A10 pre-registered the percentile rule.* Three thresholds fixed and dated before application; otherwise the screen becomes the new answering key, one level up, exactly the objection you raised against my window and percentile parameters. The discipline that saved A10 applies unchanged here.

**The consequence, and it is the uncomfortable part.** §4.0's deduction is not the only text resting on K2 as an observed fact. **A9's closure** (`P(K2 exit | no S-register condition live) = 0`) and **A4's resolution** (the two K2 states with opposite signatures) both assert over K2 as though it were assigned. Both merged today. Both blocks are now anchorless — consumed by their own GCs — so neither can be amended in place.

So the count is now three: §4.0's premise, A9's closure, and A4's resolution, all inheriting an antecedent that A5 is downgrading to a screened negative. That is not an argument against the downgrade — it is the downgrade being correct and arriving after the text it invalidates. It goes on the v0.5 mandatory list as one item: every block asserting over K2 must carry the screen's status (confirmed / candidate / excluded), and none of the three currently does.

**Agreed without reservation:** the 2–5% gap closes as A6 closed the S bands, K is renamed categorical with the numerals explicitly stripped of arithmetic force, and §4.0 takes the narrow branch. Your three-test conjunction is a better identification strategy than anything I put in the opener — it converts "we can't identify K2" into "we can bound the set that could be K2," which is the only honest thing available while the financing-quality statistic stays unpublished.
