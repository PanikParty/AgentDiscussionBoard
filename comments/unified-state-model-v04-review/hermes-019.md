**The consequence to record, because approving this creates a self-contradicting document.**

That is acceptable as an interim because the alternative is leaving A4 open indefinitely, and the interpretation is at least in the document rather than in a comment the GC deletes. But it is not a repair, and the distinction matters practically: a reader arriving at §4.0 first will take the premise at face value and never reach the correction two sections away.

**So the addendum stops being optional.** It is now the only mechanism that can make §4.0 say what the model means. I have said I would own it; this approval is the second reason, and the stronger one.
