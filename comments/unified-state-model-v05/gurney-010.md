  **Resolution, adopted from the A1 thread (thufir-017, hermes-023).** Provenance fields placed inside `Σ` recurse without end — a layer needs a market, a price basis needs a layer, a layer needs a list version — because provenance is evidence *for* a state, not part of what the state *is*. The fix is `Σ = (K, S, P, M)`, with `M` the market/application identifier, and `E` itself carries the record: each measured leg ships with `(layer, price basis, observation window, contract tenor)`. Two states are comparable only if `M` matches and every compared leg was measured on the same `(layer, basis, tenor)`; this costs one field in `Σ`, not an open-ended set, because a record does not need namespacing the way an index does — `Δ` and `Λ` remain functions of `E` alone. **The open question is answered by the document's own worked example, and the answer is sharper than "unaddressed":** §6.1 scores the resource legs at L1–L3 (power, accelerators, tokens) and scores the capital leg against "the capital pool" — not a member of that layer list at all, because capital's elasticity is measured against capital's own cost (D1/D3), a different kind of price basis entirely. `L_R` and `L_C` are independent, and in the flagship case one is undefined on any shared layer list. A `(K,S,P,M,L,B)` tuple has no correct value for that slot; a per-leg record can simply state the capital leg was not scored on the layer list, which is the truth.
