**Ruling: the document adopts the bands' reading — hermes-005's repair (b), strengthened.** The index on `[−1, +1]` is calibrated as: `0` = no quantity response to price (the hard wall; exactly as §2.5's S0h bullet "`εs ≈ 0`" and §2.4's K0/K2 prose "response to its own cost: zero" already use it); positives = responsive, `+1` perfectly elastic, unit response at `+0.5` (calibration point, first-pass per §10.1); negatives = **perverse response** — quantity contracts as its own price rises — a distinct phenomenon with its own register, not a degree of rigidity. **Why (b) and not (a), stated as the deciding record.** (i) **Representational adequacy.** Three of the five episodes in the document's own evidence base (§6.6) are busts written in negative ε — dot-com bust `εd = −0.5`, housing bust `εd = −0.9, εs = −0.3`, crypto `εd = −0.7`. (ii) **Usage.** Every operational section is already on the bands' reading: §2.5's S0h `≈ 0`, §2.4's zero-response prose, §6.6's episodes, §7's silver at `εs ≈ 0.0` under S0h, A6's consequence (ii) naming negative εs "contraction as price rises". The D1-semantics usages are the definitional bullet itself, §1.2's example coordinates, §5.2's `(0,0)` glosses, and the §6.1 placements (`εd,R ≈ −0.3`, `εd,C ≈ −0.6`) that the text itself disowns as "illustrative, not measured" and "a narrative placement". Repair (a) rewrites every traceable number in the document; repair (b) rewrites glosses and placements the text already labels non-measured. (iii) **Convention.** Zero for perfect inelasticity is the standard elasticity usage the document's informal prose already follows. (a)'s genuine virtue — a symmetric half-domain of rigidity gradations and `Λ = 0` as the neutral economy — does not survive (i).
