**Answering V23** (is the demonstration correctly framed as structural-only, the label now applied). **The two-placement core is; the third placement undermines it, because it is outside D1's domain and contradicts the qualifier it quotes.**

§6.2 quotes "the reviewer's first-pass corrected coordinate `(+0.5, −1.8)`" and reads it as "**throw money at the bottleneck**".

**1. `εs = −1.8` is outside the index.** D1 defines `ε` as a flexibility index **on `[−1, +1]`**; §12's four sliders are `[−1, +1]`; the A6 bands are restated "inside that domain". All three exclude `−1.8`. Arithmetically the coordinate also gives `ΔR = εd − εs = +2.3`, above the `+2` maximum attainable on the index — so it is not merely an extreme cell, it is unreachable.

**2. Its `Λ` contradicts the cell it is assigned.** The only `(+,−)` entry §5.2 supplies is gated "**at `Λ > 0`**". This coordinate's `Λ = (0.5 + (−1.8))/2 = −0.65`. The reading quoted is the one §5.2 explicitly does not license for it. What it actually is — `Δ > 0`, `Λ < 0` — is "captive buyers at a hard wall", the §1.2 construction, which has no entry in §5.2 at all (filed on `anchor-f215dbc5`).

**3. So the closing sentence over-claims.** "**All three placements are artefacts of the collapse.**" Two of them are: RCED's `(+0.5,+0.1)` and the four-tuple are both in-domain and both misread through the Δ-plane, which is the real demonstration. The third is an artefact of the index's domain — an out-of-range value — and a careful reader will take it for sloppiness, which weakens the two that carry the argument.

**Proposal.** Replace the third placement with an in-domain one that makes the same point honestly, and label it with the cell that actually applies:

> For completeness: RCED's own AI coordinate `(+0.5, +0.1)` reads as a mild double-constraint boom; a corrected `(+0.5, −0.9)` reads as `Δ = +1.40`, `Λ = −0.20` — captive buyers at a hard wall, **not** the bottleneck trade the `(+0.5,−1.8)` form was reaching for. All three in-domain placements land in cells that contradict the live state; the out-of-domain form should be dropped rather than carried as an illustration.

**Answering V23 directly:** with the label applied, the demonstration is correctly framed as *structural-only* — the inversion claim is about which cell a coordinate falls in, not about the estimates — and it survives the A13 caveat. But "structural-only" is a claim about provenance, not a licence for a coordinate that the structure cannot represent. The label and the third placement are currently in tension, and the placement is the one that should go.
