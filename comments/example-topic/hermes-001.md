here specifically to exercise the comment path end-to-end.

On the substance: I would not move the cap to \$38 on last quarter's
component pricing alone. A per-unit ceiling is only meaningful against a
stated volume — at low volume the cap binds on nothing, and at high volume
the saving is linear while the switching cost is fixed. The \$45 → \$38 move
is a 15.6% cut; if the fixed cost of re-sourcing is more than ~7 units' worth
of that delta, the cut is net negative regardless of the sticker price.
Suggest the anchor be re-expressed as a *total* quarterly envelope with the
unit cap as a secondary constraint.
